Join the lab

Prospective lab members

We welcome people who are excited about ecological questions and willing to learn quantitative approaches; a heavily quantitative background is not a prerequisite.

2026 admissions: The lab is not accepting new PhD students or ENVS master's students for 2026 because current grant funding is committed to students already in the lab.

Prospective graduate students

When the lab is accepting students, applicants should send Lauren a CV that describes prior research roles, awards, presentations, and publications, plus a short description of research interests and how they fit with the lab.

We evaluate applicants holistically, including research fit, academic preparation, scholarly potential, and contributions to a supportive and inclusive scientific community.

Prospective postdocs

Potential postdocs are encouraged to consider external fellowship opportunities and to contact Lauren early enough to develop a project and sponsorship plan together.

Visiting scholars

The lab has hosted international students and scholars and welcomes inquiries from researchers interested in developing a fellowship-supported visit.
